About NRIINT

THE COMPANY
BEHIND THE DETAILS.

NRI International Ltd (NRIINT) is a premium garment accessories and packaging supplies with a commitment to quality, innovation, and global partnership. Based in Uttara, Dhaka, we produce high-grade woven labels, hang tags, metal trims, elastics, tapes, drawcords, and complete packaging programs for fashion brands, buying houses, and manufacturers worldwide. Every order is shade-matched, lab-tested, and documented to exceed global retail compliance standards.

From drawing board to bulk

HOW AN ORDER RUNS

STEP 1

Design & sampling — artwork received, digitized and sampled against your tech pack.

STEP 2

Production — needle looms, press shops and packaging lines run under one roof.

STEP 3

Quality control — shade matching, elongation, strength and print checks per lot.

STEP 4

Export logistics — documentation, compliance files and consolidated shipping.
