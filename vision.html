Our Vision

A TRUSTED PARTNER,
WORLDWIDE.

To become a globally recognized and trusted partner in garment accessories and packaging solutions, setting new standards in quality, innovation, sustainability, and reliability.

Where we are heading

FROM DHAKA
TO EVERY BRAND’S SUPPLY CHAIN.

We want brands and buying houses anywhere in the world to think of NRI International first when they need labels, trims and packaging they can rely on.

Our factory in Uttara, Dhaka is where that ambition is built, one lot at a time. Every shade-matched label, every tested elastic and every correctly packed carton is a step toward becoming a partner that buyers trust without second-guessing.

We measure our progress by the standard we set for ourselves: consistent quality, ideas that move the craft forward, responsible ways of working, and a record of delivering what we promise.

The four pillars

WHAT OUR VISION STANDS ON

Quality

A standard we hold lot after lot: shade-matched, tested and documented, so buyers know what they will receive.

Innovation

Modern looms, tipping lines and print technology, and the curiosity to develop new trims with our customers.

Partnership

One accountable contact from sampling to shipped cartons, and relationships built to last beyond a single order.

Sustainability

Working toward responsible sourcing, azo-free dyeing, nickel-free plating and packaging that wastes less.
